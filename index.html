First Aid Tips
Quick steps before medical help arrives.
❤️‍🩹
CPR (Adults)
  • Check response & breathing.
  • Call emergency number / use SOS.
  • Push hard & fast in center of chest.
  • Rate: 100–120 compressions/min.
🔥
Burns
  • Cool burn under running water (20 min).
  • Do NOT apply ice or toothpaste.
  • Cover with clean, non-stick cloth.
🩸
Severe Bleeding
  • Apply firm pressure with clean cloth.
  • Keep injured part elevated if possible.
  • Do not remove soaked dressings, add more.
🤕
Head Injury
  • Keep person still, support neck.
  • Watch for vomiting, confusion, drowsiness.
  • Seek urgent medical care.
